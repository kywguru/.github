## Hi there 👋
KYW.guru is **a Revolutionary Source-Available Wallet Recovery Solution Using RFID Smart Cards**

## Introduction

KYW.guru provides an innovative, trustless way to split and recover your wallet's seed phrase:
- **Owner's Cards** for your personal secure storage
- **Keeper's Cards** held by trusted people who help you recover if needed

Through advanced mathematics and secure hardware (EAL5+ Secure Elements), KYW.guru ensures that no single card or even all Keeper's Cards can reconstruct your seed. You remain in complete control—forever.  

You can find much more info in  [the official website](https://kyw.guru) and you can put yourself [on the spot for pre-order](https://kyw.guru/pre-order) once production lines will be on!
